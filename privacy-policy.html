Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026.

Motomoto Content Agent ("the App") is a personal content tool for the owner of the MotoMoto Tech YouTube channel. This policy describes what the App accesses and how it is used.

Data the App accesses

When the owner connects their Google account, the App receives YouTube permissions to upload videos, read the channel's own video data, and read YouTube Analytics for the owner's own channel. Access is granted through Google's OAuth consent screen and can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account permissions page.

How data is used

Accessed data is used only to publish and manage the owner's own videos on their own channel. The App does not sell, share, or disclose channel data to any third party, and does not use it for advertising.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own machine, in a permission-restricted file, and are never committed to any public repository or shared with anyone.

Third parties

The App calls Google's YouTube APIs directly. No other third-party service receives channel data from the App.

Contact

Questions about this policy: motomototheyoutuber@gmail.com.
